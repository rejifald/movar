extension: YouTube filtering works again on today's markup — including the redesigned watch sidebar, Shorts shelves, playlist pages and channel tabs — and now also covers channel community Posts. Clicking a video from search results can no longer be aborted by the language rewrite (a canceled navigation used to poison the deferral's old-URL check), and returning from a video to the results page no longer costs a full-page reload blink. Google's own "Web" filter tab (`udm=web`) gets the language enforcement it was silently missing, and model chunks stop loading on sibling frontends they cannot parse (YouTube Music/Studio/Kids, Google News/Scholar/Translate).
